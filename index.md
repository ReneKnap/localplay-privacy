# Privacy Policy

**App:** Localplay (`io.github.reneknap.localplay`) — full version and free demo
**Effective date:** 2026-10-03

> This is the privacy policy for Localplay. It is linked from the app's Google Play
> listing and applies to both editions (the paid full version and the free demo).

## Summary

Localplay does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app
has **no internet permission**, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
tracking. Everything it keeps stays **on your device**, and it is used only to run
the app for you.

## What the app accesses

- **Media files in folders you choose.** When you pick a folder, the app uses
  Android's Storage Access Framework to read the audio and video files inside it,
  to list and play them and to show their tags and cover art. It does **not**
  request broad storage permissions and cannot see files outside the folders you
  grant.
- **Your microphone — only when you record a voice note.** Voice bookmarks are
  recorded only after you tap the record button and grant the microphone permission.
  Recording stops when you stop it; nothing is recorded in the background.

## What the app changes in your files

The app writes to your media files **only when you ask it to**: when you edit a
track's tags or cover art, or when you choose to write your ratings into the files.
These changes stay in the files on your device.

## What the app stores on your device

All of the following is stored **locally**, in the app's own storage, and never
uploaded:

- the folders you picked, your playlists and collections;
- your settings (theme, equalizer profiles, playback options);
- your playback state: the queue and history, and where you stopped in longer
  tracks (resume positions and watched progress for videos);
- **play statistics** — how often and how long each track was played and when it
  was last played. You can turn this off and clear it under Settings;
- **ratings, tags, and bookmarks** you create, including your **voice-note
  recordings**;
- a cache of the library scan and of cover thumbnails, so the library opens
  quickly;
- how many times the app has been started and whether you have dismissed the
  "Rate the app" hint — used only to decide whether to show that hint, never to
  identify you.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this. Android's built-in **Auto Backup** — a
system feature you control in your device's backup settings, not something the app
initiates — may include this data in your device's backup to your Google account.
The app itself transmits nothing and has no access to that backup.

## Backup files you create

Under Settings you can **export** your app data to a file or **share** it through
Android's share sheet. This happens only when you start it, and the file goes only
where you choose to save or send it. Importing a backup file reads it locally.

## Network, analytics, and ads

- The app declares **no internet permission** and makes no network requests.
- There is **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDK.**

The only outbound actions are **initiated by you**: the "Rate the app" button — and,
in the free demo, the "Upgrade" button — opens the app's Google Play listing. Once it
opens, Google Play handles it under **its own** privacy policy; Localplay sends it
nothing about you.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to show the playback notification with
  transport controls.
- **Foreground service** (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`)
  — to keep playing in the background.
- **Microphone** (`RECORD_AUDIO`) — only for recording voice bookmarks, asked for the
  first time you record.
- **Folder access** (granted by you via the Storage Access Framework) — to read and
  play, and on request edit, the files in the folders you pick.

It requests **no** internet, location, contacts, camera, or broad storage
permissions.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. You can delete individual items in
the app (playlists, bookmarks, tags, ratings, play statistics), or remove all of it by
clearing the app's storage in Android's settings or by uninstalling the app. Because
nothing is ever sent to the developer, there is no copy elsewhere to delete.

## Children

Localplay is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal data from anyone.
